# VinesFlow FBR Digital Invoicing Marketing Campaign

Updated: 2 August 2026

## Campaign objective

Generate qualified demo conversations with sales-tax-registered Pakistani businesses that need a
clearer way to prepare, validate, submit and retain FBR digital invoices.

The campaign should lead with FBR Digital Invoicing. Inventory, sales, purchases, accounting and
reports are supporting reasons to choose VinesFlow instead of a single-purpose invoicing tool.

Primary conversion:

- Book an FBR setup demo
- Start a qualified WhatsApp conversation

Do not optimize the campaign for likes, reactions or general website traffic.

## Positioning

### Core promise

> FBR Digital Invoicing, made simple.

### Supporting promise

> Validate, submit and print invoices with the returned IRN and QR code—while sales, stock and
> accounts stay connected.

### Brand role

VinesFlow is the business system used to prepare and manage invoices. Onboarding and production
integration must use the approved route applicable to the customer, such as PRAL or another
FBR-licensed integrator.

Never advertise VinesFlow as an FBR-approved product, official FBR partner or licensed integrator
unless that status is formally obtained and can be substantiated.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Sales-tax-registered companies and sole proprietors
- Distributors, wholesalers, importers and product-based businesses
- Owners, finance managers, accountants and tax teams
- Businesses currently preparing invoices manually or across disconnected software
- Businesses entering FBR sandbox testing or preparing for production invoicing

Qualification questions:

1. Is the business registered for sales tax?
2. What type of products or services does it sell?
3. Approximately how many sales invoices are issued each month?
4. Is it currently using PRAL or another licensed integrator?
5. Has it received sandbox or production credentials?
6. What software is currently used for invoicing, stock and accounting?

The supplied logo must be placed as an original asset. Do not redraw, reinterpret, recolour, crop,
warp or regenerate it. Keep generous clear space around it.

### Colour direction

Dominant colours:

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Warm off-white: approximately `#FCFDFC`
- Pale mint surface: `#EEF3F0`
- Charcoal text: approximately `#1E2F2E`
- Muted slate text: approximately `#60736F`

Logo colours:

- Deep teal: `#0F766E`
- Fresh green: `#22C55E`
- Soft lime: `#86EFAC`

Keep the teal-to-lime gradient inside the original logo asset only. Use the listed colours as flat,
solid accents elsewhere. Do not apply gradients to the CTA, text, invoice, cards or background.
The design should remain mostly white. Do not introduce blue, purple or red gradients, metallic
effects or a government-document aesthetic.

### Typography

- Primary family: Figtree
- Weights: 750–800 for headline, 600–700 for labels and action line, 400–500 for supporting copy
- Optional technical details: IBM Plex Mono
- Headline tracking: slightly tight
- Labels: small uppercase with generous letter spacing
- Never horizontally or vertically scale type. Keep font width at 100%.
- Feed headline: 72–84 px, no more than three lines, line-height 0.98–1.05.
- Supporting copy: 27–32 px, no more than three short lines, line-height 1.35–1.45.
- Keep each text block to a natural reading width; do not justify text or force it to fill a box.

If Figtree is unavailable, use Inter or a clean geometric sans-serif. Do not use serif, handwritten,
condensed display or decorative fonts.

### Shape and image language

- Clean white canvas with restrained pale-mint geometry
- Rounded cards, approximately 12–18 px corner radius
- Thin neutral or pale-mint borders
- Soft, wide shadows with low opacity
- One invoice card as the visual focal point
- Small validation check, IRN and QR details as proof points
- Minimal line icons; no 3D cartoons
- Large areas of negative space

### Layout

1. Place the original logo and VinesFlow wordmark at the top-left.
2. Place the small FBR eyebrow below the brand row.
3. Use the upper-left area for a compact headline and two short supporting lines. Do not stretch
   the text block to fill the available height.
4. Place the real sample FBR invoice on the lower-right as an upright floating card.
5. Preserve the invoice's original aspect ratio exactly. Scale proportionally from the corners;
   never warp, widen, narrow, skew or stretch it.
6. Keep the QR and IRN area visible, but do not make the QR large enough to look like the action.
7. Use one flat pale-mint oval or panel behind the invoice; no background gradient.
8. Put the plain-text action line and `vinesflow.com` toward the lower-left.
9. Put the legal qualifier along the bottom in small but readable text.

The poster must still be understandable at phone-feed size. Do not reproduce the entire dashboard
or add a long feature list.

## Recommended generation prompt — visual foundation only

Image generators frequently misspell brand and compliance copy. For the most professional result,
generate only the visual foundation, then place the original logo, real invoice and exact text in
Figma, Canva or Photoshop.

```text
Create a polished light-theme background and layout foundation for a 4:5 Pakistani B2B SaaS
advertisement, 1080 × 1350 pixels.

Use a clean white and warm off-white canvas with very pale mint panels. Add restrained flat-colour
accents in #0F766E, #22C55E and #86EFAC, inspired by a teal-to-green-to-lime botanical technology logo.
Use dark charcoal only as a placeholder colour. Include generous negative space, rounded 14 px
cards, thin pale-mint borders and soft low-opacity shadows.

Reserve a clear brand area at the top-left, a large headline and supporting-copy area across the
upper-left, a small plain-text action area below it, and a lower-right product-proof area sized for
an upright A4 invoice screenshot at its natural aspect ratio. Add one flat pale-mint shape behind
the product area. Keep the composition minimal, balanced and premium.

Do not render any words, letters, logos, QR codes, invoice data, buttons, badges or interface
screenshots. Do not use a dark background, blue, purple, red gradients, people, office photography,
coins, handshakes, calculators, government buildings, flags, 3D cartoons, stretched placeholders
or excessive decoration.
```

After generation, manually composite:

1. The original logo asset
2. The exact VinesFlow wordmark
3. The real invoice screenshot
4. The approved copy
5. The plain-text action line, `vinesflow.com` and qualifier

## Approval checklist

Before publishing the ad:

- Use the original VinesFlow logo without modification
- Use only fictional customer, NTN, STRN, invoice and contact details in screenshots
- Confirm the QR code in the demo invoice cannot expose a real customer record
- Confirm the CTA destination works on mobile
- Confirm someone can answer WhatsApp enquiries promptly
- Confirm the demo environment is ready
- Confirm price, trial and onboarding terms
- Confirm all FBR claims match the current product and integration arrangement
- Preview Feed, Story and Reel crops in Ads Manager
- Add Meta campaign or account spending limits
